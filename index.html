Home / Loft Conversions

Loft Conversions

The space above your head, turned into a bedroom, office or bathroom that actually gets used.

What's included

Dormer, hip-to-gable & mansard

We assess your roof structure and headroom, then recommend the conversion type that gets you the most usable space for the least disruption — and manage building regulations and structural work throughout.

  • Structural steels & floor strengthening
  • Dormer or roof-line alterations
  • Staircase design & installation
  • Insulation, electrics & full finish

Types of loft conversion we build

The right option depends on your roof shape and how much headroom you have.

Dormer conversion

Extends the roof vertically at the back to create full head-height and floor space.

Hip-to-gable conversion

Straightens a sloped side roof into a vertical gable wall, adding significant space.

Mansard conversion

Replaces most of the roof slope for maximum space — often used on terraced houses.

Rooflight / Velux conversion

The simplest option where existing headroom already allows a usable room.

How it runs

The same four stages, every time, so you always know what's happening and when.

STAGE 1

Survey & design

Roof survey, headroom check, and conversion type agreed.

STAGE 2

Fixed quote

A written, itemised quote with no surprise additions later.

STAGE 3

Build

Structural work, roof alterations, and staircase fitted.

STAGE 4

Finish & handover

Insulation, electrics, decoration and final sign-off.
